Waterline

A teaching model of rain, tide and coastal protection.

Open the project ↗Source code ↗

Who it is for

Teams and classes studying how rainfall, tide and local measures change risk in a fictional district.

What it does

Adjust rain and tide, place pumps, rain gardens and barriers, and watch how water spreads across blocks, roads and people. A baseline run can be saved and compared with a protected run.

What you get

A scenario comparison and a note with the starting conditions.

Limits

A simplified teaching model, not a flood forecast.
