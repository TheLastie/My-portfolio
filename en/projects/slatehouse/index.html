Slatehouse

A shooting day: scenes, light, moves and call times.

Open the project ↗Source code ↗

Who it is for

Small film crews putting together a shooting schedule.

What it does

Scenes are grouped by location and light, and the order, moves and cast call times are worked out. Day and night scenes, durations set from your own estimates, and checks for conflicts (sunset, shift length, number of moves).

What you get

A call sheet for the crew and a working copy of the project.

Limits

Sunset limits use a default time. Weather is typed in by hand and is not pulled from a forecast.
