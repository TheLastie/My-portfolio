Silva · Plant Growth

Plant growth variants for a scene or a plot.

Open the project ↗Source code ↗

Who it is for

Environment artists, garden designers and teachers who need to compare plant forms.

What it does

Oak, birch, fern and monstera. Age, light, water and wind visibly change trunk, branching and crown. Samples can be labelled and gathered into a comparison sheet.

What you get

A labelled concept sheet as PNG for discussion or hand-off.

Limits

The model visualises form; it does not predict biological growth.
