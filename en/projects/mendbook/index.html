Mendbook

A passport for your belongings: purchases, warranties, repairs.

Open the project ↗Source code ↗

Who it is for

Owners of devices and things who decide whether to repair or replace.

What it does

Item cards with purchase date, price, warranty and service history, check reminders, and a monthly-cost comparison between repair and replacement. Data stays on your device and the app works offline.

What you get

A local item passport, reminders and a JSON backup.

Limits

The repair-versus-replace figure is indicative: it ignores downtime, convenience and disposal.
