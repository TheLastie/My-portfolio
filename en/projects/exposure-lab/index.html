Exposure Lab

An exposure calculator with a test scene.

Open the project ↗Source code ↗

Who it is for

Photographers who work out exposure for a scene and check how camera automation behaves.

What it does

Aperture, shutter and ISO change the brightness and depth of field of the scene. It has an Auto mode, lighting presets, focus and white balance settings, and shows scene EV and deviation in stops.

What you get

Chosen shot settings and a field note you can save.

Limits

A teaching model of exposure, not a replacement for a light meter.
